## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated (Aaryav approved the plan): Astro 5 static site, hand-tuned CSS tokens, self-hosted fonts. Public GitHub repo `aaryavvatts-lab/neurogidas-web`, auto-deployed by Vercel to neurogidas.vercel.app.

## Users

- Eastview High School students deciding whether to try the club, usually on a phone, often after seeing a flyer or an Instagram post.
- Current members checking what is on this Wednesday, where to find materials, and how the year builds.
- Adults judging credibility: teachers, parents, miRcore, college readers, and district people who might start a chapter elsewhere.

## Product Purpose

The public home of NeuroGIDAS, Eastview's combined neuroscience and genetics research club. It gets students to show up on a Wednesday, gives members one place for the schedule and resources, and shows adults that the club does real research. Success is a student joining the GroupMe or walking into B327.

## Positioning

Two tracks under one club. Neuroscience weeks are hands-on brain science. GIDAS weeks are real computational biology using the same public databases researchers use, and members finish with their own abstract presented at the miRcore conference in April. Members don't need any experience to start.

## Operating Context

- Meetings are Wednesdays 2:45 to 3:30 PM in Room B327 (Mr. Meyer's room). Weeks alternate between Neuro and GIDAS.
- Members join through the GroupMe (https://groupme.com/join_group/116138825/N4amjZQj), Instagram @neurogidas.evhs, or by emailing neurogidas@gmail.com.
- GIDAS is the high school program of miRcore, a nonprofit affiliated with the University of Michigan that has more than 130 chapters.
- Cancer is this year's research focus across the network.
- The year's events are the Brain Bee, the Genes and Health Contest (January), Brain Awareness Week (March), and the miRcore High School Research Conference (April).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is static. It has no forms, accounts, or backend, and joining happens on external links.
- The schedule lives in one data file, and the "next meeting" picks itself by date.
- Future meeting dates are tentative and must be labeled that way.
- Undecided: the specific disease members voted on. Do not name one.

## Brand Commitments

- The name is always written "NeuroGIDAS".
- Colors are indigo #5B4BD6, teal #0E8F84, salmon #E86A55, ink #1E222B, and cream #FBFBF8.
- The emblem is a neuron sitting on a DNA helix.
- Writing rules:
  - No em dashes anywhere.
  - Use a plain, human, high-school voice.
  - Don't add filler perks or snack lines.
  - Leave space empty rather than padding it.
- Footer disclaimer: this is a student-led group, and its views are not necessarily those of Eastview High School or District 196.

## Product Principles

1. Show up Wednesday: every page makes the next step obvious.
2. Real over impressive: only claim what the club actually does.
3. Beginners welcome, research real: make the bar to join low and the substance deep.
4. Easy to keep true: content lives in data so the officers can update it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
